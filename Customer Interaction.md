# Customer Interview Notes

## Portfolio Manager

As part of the initial planning for the Portfolio Manager project, we had a discussion with the customer to understand their requirements and expectations from the application.

The main purpose of the interview was to understand how the customer currently manages their investments, the difficulties they face, and what features they would find useful in a portfolio management application.

The customer is looking for a simple and convenient way to track different investments from one place. The discussion mainly focused on the types of investments they want to manage, how they want to view their portfolio performance, support for investments across different countries and currencies, wealth-related feel-good milestones, and the overall experience they expect from the application.

## Key Points from the Discussion

- The customer wants to manage different types of investments such as **stocks, ETFs, Fixed Deposits (FDs), and cash** from one place. More investment types can be considered in the future.

- They would like a **single dashboard** that gives a quick view of their overall portfolio, including total amount invested, current portfolio value, overall profit/loss, percentage return, and today's gain/loss.

- The customer is interested in investing across different markets such as **India, the US, the UK, Europe, and China**. Since investments can be in different currencies, they would like to see values in both the **original currency and their preferred/base currency**.

- An important requirement is to show **realized and unrealized profit/loss separately**. This would help the customer understand the profit already earned from sold investments as well as the current profit or loss on investments they still hold.

- The customer would like to see **portfolio performance visually**, using simple graphs or charts. They also want to understand how their investments are distributed across different asset types.

- The dashboard should provide a **daily wealth overview**, so that when the customer opens the application, they can quickly understand their current financial position without going through multiple pages.

- The customer also suggested adding **wealth-related milestones** to make portfolio tracking more engaging. For example, when the portfolio crosses a certain value, the application could show a milestone or compare the portfolio value with something aspirational, such as a luxury car.

- Overall, the customer expects the application to be **simple, modern, visually appealing, and easy to understand**, while providing the important information needed to track their investments and financial progress.

These points will be considered while deciding the features and design for the initial version of the Portfolio Manager. Further improvements can be made based on customer feedback during the development process.
